Frequently asked

How do I download for offline?

Tap the download icon on any detail page. Downloads work on Basic plan and above.

Can I share my account?

Family plan supports up to 6 profiles. Sharing outside your household violates our terms.

Why can't I stream in 4K?

4K requires the Premium plan, a compatible device, and at least 25 Mbps connection.
